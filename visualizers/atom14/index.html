Important distinction

A slot is not a universal atom type

Slots 0–3 are stable across residues. Later slots are compact and context-dependent: slot 5 is CG in many residues, OG in serine, SG in cysteine, and padding in glycine and alanine. Unknown residues use fourteen padded slots.
